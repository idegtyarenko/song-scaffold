Why the steps aren’t all 5 BPM

Gebrian’s instructions say to click up “by 5s”, a constant increment. This app shrinks each increment as the tempo rises, because difficulty near your top speed is asymptotic rather than proportional: 140→150 costs far more than 75→80 even though it is the smaller jump. The long strides belong low down, where you are nowhere near your limit. Type a step count yourself to stay closer to her version.
